A tiny program for the [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

It writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — a single sentence about a
small moment of wonder in everyday life:

> The lift doors opened on a stranger humming my dead grandfather's favourite song, and for one floor the whole building felt like his kitchen again.

The surprise is in the phrasing itself: an ordinary lift ride turns, for
one floor, into a dead man's kitchen. It lands in one read, stays a single
clear sentence, and is grounded in something real and specific — a
stranger's hum, a song, one floor — rather than floating free of the
everyday.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
